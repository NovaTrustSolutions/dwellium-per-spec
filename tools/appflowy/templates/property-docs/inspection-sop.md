# Unit Inspection SOP

Standard procedure for routine, move-out and drive-by inspections at Woodland
Parc Townhomes and Riverwood Club Apartments. (Process doc, not legal advice.)

## Schedule

| Type | When | Notice |
|---|---|---|
| Routine interior | Every 6 months per unit | Written notice per the lease (we give 48 hours) |
| Move-out | Within 3 business days of keys back | None needed — unit is vacant |
| Drive-by / exterior | Monthly per property | None |
| Post-work-order spot check | Within a week of any major repair | Coordinate with resident |

Georgia note: state law doesn't set a fixed entry-notice period — the lease
governs. Follow the lease's notice clause every time; never enter occupied
units unannounced except a genuine emergency (fire, flood, gas).

## Routine interior inspection

1. Send the notice; log it in the unit file.
2. Walk every room with the checklist: leaks under sinks and around toilets, HVAC filter, smoke/CO detectors (test buttons), signs of pests, unauthorized occupants or pets, caulk and grout condition, balcony/patio condition.
3. Photograph anything noteworthy — file in Photo Vault under the unit.
4. Create work orders for anything found; tag safety items URGENT (see the maintenance escalation policy).
5. Log the inspection date and outcome in the unit file.

## Move-out inspection

1. Walk the unit against the signed **move-in** condition form — compare line by line.
2. Photograph every room the same way as move-in for a before/after record.
3. Classify each issue: normal wear and tear (owner cost) vs damage beyond wear (deductible from deposit).
4. Georgia deposit deadline: itemized list of deductions and refund due to the former resident **within one month** of lease termination / surrender (O.C.G.A. § 44-7-34). Missing the deadline risks forfeiting the right to keep any of it.
5. Turn findings into a turnover work-order list (paint, clean, repairs) with target ready date.

## Drive-by / exterior

Check: roofs and gutters, siding, common-area lighting, landscaping condition,
dumpster area, parking (abandoned or unregistered vehicles), pool/gate hardware
in season. Photograph issues; open work orders same day.
