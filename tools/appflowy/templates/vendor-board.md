# Vendor Board

Kanban of active vendors by trade for Woodland Parc Townhomes and Riverwood
Club Apartments. Each card below is an **example — replace with your real
vendors** (`templates/IMPORT.md` explains how to turn these columns into an
AppFlowy Board).

Card fields to keep per vendor: contact name, phone, email, COI (insurance)
expiry, rate, properties served, last job + date.
