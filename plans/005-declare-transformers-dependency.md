## Why this matters

`speakerEmbedder.ts` imports `@huggingface/transformers` directly, but it is **not**
declared in `qualia-shell/package.json`. It only resolves today because
`@moonshine-ai/moonshine-js` pulls it in transitively (visible in `package-lock.json`).
That's a phantom dependency: the version the app gets is dictated by moonshine, and if
moonshine bumps its range — or is removed (see plan 009) — the speaker-embedding path
silently breaks at runtime. Declaring it makes the dependency explicit, version-pinned,
and independent of moonshine.
