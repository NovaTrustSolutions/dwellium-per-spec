# Maintenance Escalation Policy

How maintenance requests are triaged at Woodland Parc Townhomes and Riverwood
Club Apartments. Georgia context: the landlord's repair duty is statutory
(O.C.G.A. § 44-7-13) and can't be waived in the lease — habitability items are
never "when we get to it". (Process doc, not legal advice.)

## Tiers and response targets

| Tier | Examples | Respond | Resolve target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EMERGENCY** | Fire, gas smell, major flood, sewage backup, no heat below ~40°F outside, security breach (door/window won't secure), CO alarm | Immediately, 24/7 | Make safe same day; permanent fix ASAP |
| **URGENT** | No hot water, no AC in summer heat, refrigerator dead, only toilet clogged, roof leak into unit, lockout | Same business day | 24–48 hours |
| **ROUTINE** | Dripping faucet, appliance glitch, torn screen, interior door issue | 1 business day | 7 days |
| **COSMETIC / DEFERRED** | Paint touch-ups, scuffed trim, upgrade requests | Acknowledge in 2 business days | Next turnover or scheduled batch |

Gas smell or suspected CO: tell the resident to leave the unit and call the gas
company / 911 first, then dispatch — before anything else.

## Escalation path

1. **Intake** — request arrives (portal, call, text, or found on inspection). Log it as a work order with unit, description, photos, permission-to-enter.
2. **Triage** — assign a tier (table above) within the response window. When in doubt between two tiers, pick the higher.
3. **Dispatch** — EMERGENCY: on-call maintenance or emergency vendor from the Vendor Board, now. URGENT/ROUTINE: assign to staff or the trade's primary vendor with the resolve target on the ticket.
4. **Spend authority** — staff may approve up to $500 per ticket; $500–$1,500 needs Andy's OK; over $1,500 or anything structural needs Andy plus an owner heads-up. Emergencies: make-safe spending is pre-authorized, notify Andy same day.
5. **Verify** — no ticket closes without after-photos (Photo Vault) and, for URGENT+, a resident confirmation.
6. **Escalate stalls** — any ticket past its resolve target gets flagged daily to Andy; a second missed target moves the job to a different vendor.

## Habitability watch list

Treat as URGENT-or-higher even if the resident is casual about it: heat, hot
water, running water, sewage, electrical hazards, roof leaks, broken exterior
locks, smoke/CO detectors. These are § 44-7-13 repair-duty items — document
response times on the ticket.
